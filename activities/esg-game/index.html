Game rules ▼
Goal Balance economic growth (flock size) against social reputation (sustainability points) while keeping the ecosystem from collapsing. If pasture health reaches 0, a crisis vote is triggered.
Beginner flow (play it this way) Each round has three steps: read the current random event, choose an action for each of the 5 factions (every card needs one), and let the system resolve the round and move on (or select “End round” yourself). If the Industrial Syndicate wants to trade, trade first and then choose its action.
Available actions
  • Expand: +1 sheep, −2 health (−1 for the first expansion by the Development community)
  • Maintain: no change to the flock, −1 health
  • Restore: −1 sheep, +1 sustainability point, +2 health (+3 for the Eco-pioneer)
Natural dynamics
  • Baseline natural decline: −1 health per round
  • Overload penalty: an extra −1 when total sheep ≥ health × 1.5
  • Severe overload: an extra −2 when total sheep ≥ health × 2
Collapse and endings The game runs 4 normal rounds (up to round 6 if a collapse happens in between). When health reaches 0, a vote is triggered: choosing "Take what is left" immediately grants 3 sheep, resets SP to 0 and scores the flock at half value; choosing "Scientific restoration" restores health and scores normally. Normal and restoration scoring: sheep + SP×2.
